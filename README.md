# My Favorite Series: Stranger Things

**Stranger Things** is an American science fiction horror series created by the Duffer Brothers.  
It is set in the 1980s in the fictional town of Hawkins, Indiana.

---

## Main Characters
- **Eleven (El)** – A girl with psychokinetic abilities  
- **Mike Wheeler** – Leader of the friend group  
- **Dustin Henderson** – The funny and clever friend  
- **Lucas Sinclair** – Courageous and loyal  
- **Will Byers** – The boy who disappears in season 1

---

## Why I Love It
1. Engaging and suspenseful storyline  
2. Nostalgic 1980s vibes  
3. Strong friendships and character development  
4. Creepy, thrilling supernatural elements  

---

## Favorite Episodes
- *Chapter One: The Vanishing of Will Byers*  
- *Chapter Eight: The Upside Down*  
- *Chapter Four: The Body*

---

## Fun Fact
The show has a famous homage to 80s movies like **E.T.**, **The Goonies**, and **Ghostbusters**.
